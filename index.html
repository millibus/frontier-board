How to read this board

Every figure here was pulled from a live leaderboard or a provider's own publication on or before the snapshot date in the header — each benchmark shows its own source date on the Benchmarks tab and below. Nothing is estimated, interpolated, or filled in from memory — a model with no score on a benchmark shows a dash, not a guess.

Three provenance levels are marked on every number. Independent means a third party ran the eval (Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, Scale, Snorkel, ARC Prize, the SWE-bench team, OpenRouter, the WeirdML author). Vendor-run means the number comes from the model maker's own announcement or model card, or from a competitor's comparison table — useful, but not neutral. Unverified means it was found in one place with no upstream citation. Numbers that appear only on aggregator sites are never used.

What lives in the data block

Everything on this page is driven by five blocks in the page's script. BENCH defines each benchmark — its label, source URL, source date, unit, and whether higher is better. EFFORT holds each model's reasoning-effort levels, PRICES its list prices, and CHANGELOG the history shown under What changed. MODELS is one entry per model:

  • name, prov (provider), tier (frontier, challenger, or legacy), open (true for open weights), rel (release date), note.
  • s — the scores map. Each entry is benchId: [value, quality, note] where quality is ind, ven, or unv and the note (who measured it, which variant) is optional. Adding a benchmark to a model is one line; adding a whole benchmark means one BENCH entry plus the scores.

Checks before publishing

node scripts/check-data.mjs checks the data for consistency: score shapes, model names across blocks, source dates against release dates, and notes that quote an Index score the model no longer has. node scripts/smoke.mjs opens the page in headless Chromium and clicks through every tab.

What to refresh, and how often

  • On a release — the new model plus whatever it has on day one, which is usually vendor-run only. Independent numbers land one to three weeks later.
  • Monthly — Arena Elo and Artificial Analysis move continuously; ARC Prize and WeirdML add models as they test them.
  • Rarely — SWE-bench Pro and METR update in bursts and are currently months behind the frontier.
